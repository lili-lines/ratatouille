#### 4 - Inertial sensing
The mouse 🐭 needs to know two things: how far it has come, and which way it's looking. So 1 sensor for each. <br>
🔎: And 2 sensors are complementary: the encoders give distance but drift when a wheel slips, while the gyro gives heading but drifts over time. Fusing them gives a stable position + heading.*

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**Wheel encoders** <br>
×2, hall, built into the N20 motors, resolution CPR (à mesurer 📏) <br>
IN: 3.3V ← Teensy, wheel rotation <br>
OUT: quadrature pulses 3.3V → Teensy, 0.02A <br>
🔎: N20 = low resolution, read both channels (A+B) to keep precision, they lie when a wheel slips
</div>
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**Gyro / IMU** <br>
MPU-6050 / GY-521, 6-axis (gyro + accelerometer), 21×16 mm <br>
IN: 3.3V ← Teensy <br>
OUT: I2C data 3.3V, address 0x68 → Teensy, 0.004A <br>
🔎: I2C only, no SPI, the gyro drifts slowly over time
</div>
<img src="{{ '/assets/img/gyro_MPU-6050.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="MPU-6050 IMU" style="flex:0 0 160px; max-width:160px; border-radius:6px;">
</div>

**Σ A (4) inertial = 0.024 $A$**

#### 5 - Clean signal chain
Motor noise has 4 defenders.

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**Reservoir capacitor** <br>
≥ 470 µF electrolytic, Ø10×12 mm <br>
7.4V rail, across Voltage Motor (VM) at the driver <br>
absorbs the motor current spikes → stable VM <br>
rated ≥ 16V (25V best), polarised: + on VM, − on GND
</div>
<img src="{{ '/assets/img/cap_470uF.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Reservoir capacitor 470uF" style="flex:0 0 160px; max-width:100px; border-radius:6px;">
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**RC filter** <br>
R 1 kΩ + C 100 nF <br>
IN/OUT: noisy analog 0–3.3V ← AS5600, clean analog → Teensy ADC <br>
cut-off frequency (fc) = 1/(2πRC) = 1.6kHz, + the whisker's useful speed, - the PWM noise <br>

🔎: if the pick R or C wrong, the cut-off moves: <br>
. fc too low → cut the whisker signal <br>
. fc too high → let the noise through <br>

</div>
<img src="{{ '/assets/img/RC_filter.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="RC filter" style="flex:0 0 160px; max-width:160px; border-radius:6px;">
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**Decoupling** <br>
100 nF per chip, 3.3V rail at each chip <br>
stable local 3.3V → that chip, sit a **few mm** of the chip's VDD+/GND- pins <br>
🔎: each chip has its own decoupling capacitor
</div>
<img src="{{ '/assets/img/Condensateur_decouplage_100nF.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Decoupling capacitor 100nF" style="flex:0 0 160px; max-width:160px; border-radius:6px;">
</div>

<div style="display:flex; gap:16px; align-items:center;" markdown="1">
<div style="flex:1;" markdown="1">
**Wiring** <br> 
Star ground, all GND returns one single common point <br>
never daisy-chain the motor GND through the sensor GND
</div>
<img src="{{ '/assets/img/start_ground.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Star ground wiring" style="flex:0 0 160px; max-width:160px; border-radius:6px;">
</div>

3D-print parts, everything here is mechanical.

- **Chassis**: carries every other part.
- **Motor brackets ×2**: hold the two N20 motors. The two axes must be parallel and at the same height, or the robot curves instead of going straight.
- **Whisker module ×2**: carry the AS5600 + pivot + magnet. Must hold the magnet 0.5–3 mm above the AS5600 centre, on the pivot axis.
- **Screws**: use nylon (nyloc) nuts: plain nuts unscrew themselves with the vibrations.
- **Wiring**: Dupont + perfboard, connects everything. Keep motor wires away from sensor wires + twist them, or the noise couples back in.

## Total current draw Σ $A$ & power chain 🔋

| Section | Σ $A$ |
|---|---|
| 1. Brain & power | 0.12 |
| 2. Movement | 3.01 |
| 3. Analog whiskers | 0.03 |
| 4. Inertial sensing | 0.024 |
| 5. Clean signal chain | 0 |
| 6. Chassis & mechanics | 0 |
| **TOTAL (peak)** | **≈ 3.2 $A$** |

The total is dominated by the motors at peak/stall (~3 $A$) — that's what sizes the battery (C-rating), the slide switch, the 2 A fuse and the wiring. Logic + all sensors together draw < 0.2 $A$.

Power chain <br>
7.4 $V$ (LiPo) → fuse → switch → motors (VM), and → MP1584 → 5 $V$ → Teensy → 3.3 $V$ → sensors.
